Pokémon by Dreamaker

There are tons of hacks out there that completely change up the Pokémon experience to make something new.
But what if you just wanted something that attempts to improve upon the great game that is already there?
Pokémon by Dreamaker is my attempt at doing just that. A well-organized version and most of all it is free!
