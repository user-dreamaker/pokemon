Type traits

Besides the damage multipliers above, some types come with their own immunities and stat bonuses.

Type Notes
Fire Fire-type Pokémon cannot become burned.
Grass Grass-type Pokémon are unaffected by Leech Seed, Poison Powder, Stun Spore, Sleep Powder, and Spore.
Electric Electric-type Pokémon cannot become paralyzed.
Flying Flying-type Pokémon take no damage from Spikes around their side.
Rock Rock-type Pokémon take no damage from sandstorm, and their Special Defense increases during sandstorm.
Poison Poison-type Pokémon cannot become poisoned or badly poisoned.
Ground Ground-type Pokémon are unaffected by Thunder Wave and take no damage from Electric-type moves.
Ice Ice-type Pokémon cannot be frozen. During snow, their Defense is increased by 50%.
Ghost Ghost-type Pokémon cannot be prevented from fleeing by moves that trap the target.
Steel Steel-type Pokémon take no damage from sandstorm, cannot become poisoned or badly poisoned.
